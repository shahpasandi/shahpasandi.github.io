about

I am a Splunk engineer. I design, deploy and automate Splunk environments, from a single instance to distributed and clustered deployments, and I develop add-ons that bring new data sources into Splunk in a clean, CIM-aligned way.

I also come from the SOC and blue-team side, so I know the tools and techniques analysts use every day, and I build the platform with that in mind. Earlier in my career I worked on network security, malware triage and some forensics. These are not my specialty, but they still shape how I think about data and detection.

projects

  • TI-Mirror/ A lightweight, air-gapped threat intelligence mirror for Splunk ES. It downloads 20+ free TI feeds, normalizes them at the edge and serves them over an ACL-protected Nginx endpoint, so the Search Head never needs internet access. Feeds are defined in YAML and refreshed on a schedule. Python · Nginx · YAML · Splunk ES
  • Syslog-Relay/ A production-grade syslog relay for Splunk that writes logs to disk before forwarding them with a Universal Forwarder, so nothing is lost during Splunk outages. Includes tuned rsyslog, log rotation, cgroup limits for the UF and automated cleanup. Tested with FortiGate, Sophos and Cisco devices. Shell · rsyslog · Splunk UF · Ubuntu
  • SmarterMail-addon/ A Splunk Technology Add-on for SmarterMail logs, aligned with the CIM Email data model for Splunk ES. Covers SMTP, IMAP, POP and delivery logs, with field extractions, normalization lookups and saved searches for failed-login monitoring. Also published on Splunkbase. Splunk TA · CIM · SPL · Splunkbase
